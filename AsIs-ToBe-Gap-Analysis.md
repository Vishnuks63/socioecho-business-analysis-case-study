# As-Is / To-Be and Gap Analysis

*The As-Is state is derived from the problem statement in the [BRD](Business-Requirements-Document.md). It is a qualitative baseline, not a measured one.*

| Area | As-Is | Pain Point / Gap | To-Be (SocioEcho) | Requirement | Expected Benefit |
|------|-------|------------------|-------------------|-------------|------------------|
| Finding a mentor | Students ask around informally | Do not know whom to approach | Search and recommendations by category and expertise | FR-01, FR-02, FR-04 | Faster, more relevant connections |
| Communicating a need | Ad hoc messages with no structure | Needs are unclear; mentors cannot filter | Structured request with category and details | FR-05, FR-06 | Clear, trackable requests |
| Mentor visibility | Mentors do not know who needs help | Students needing help go unnoticed | Mentors view relevant open requests | FR-07 | Reduced response time (BO-03) |
| Alumni effort | Repeated individual answers | Limited alumni time | Share a resource once for many students | FR-08 | More students supported per alumnus (BO-04) |
| Resources | Spread across websites, portals, social groups | Hard to find and trust | Central categorized library | FR-03, FR-08 | Single source of resources (BO-02) |
| Events | Scattered announcements | Students miss opportunities | Central event board | FR-10 | Higher event visibility (BO-05) |
| Control and safety | No academic context; unmoderated content | Misuse, toxic or unsafe posts | AI screening before publication plus human review of reported posts (implemented) | FR-11, FR-12, NFR-04 | Safe, trusted platform (BO-06) |

## Process Improvement Opportunities
1. **Service expectation for responses.** The simulated data shows an average response time of about 19 hours. Defining a target (for example 24 hours) and tracking requests that exceed it would turn the average into a managed measure.
2. **Unresolved requests.** 20% of simulated requests were unresolved. Analyzing these by category and mentor type would show whether the cause is matching, mentor availability or request quality.
3. **Placement demand.** Placement is the most requested category, so a curated placement resource set would reduce repeat questions.
