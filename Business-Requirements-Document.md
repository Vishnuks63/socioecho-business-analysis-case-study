# Business Requirements Document — SocioEcho

## 1. Project Overview
SocioEcho is a college-focused social networking and mentorship platform connecting students, seniors, alumni, faculty/mentors and event organizers. The platform supports academic guidance, career development, mentorship, networking, resource sharing and relevant events.

## 2. Problem Statement
Students may struggle to identify whom to approach for academic, career, placement and project guidance. Seniors and alumni may lack visibility into which students need help and what type of help is required. Alumni may also have limited time to respond individually, while useful resources are distributed across websites, portals and social groups.

SocioEcho addresses this problem by centralizing relevant connections, mentorship requests, resources, sessions and opportunities.

## 3. Business Objectives
- **BO-01:** Enable students to communicate academic, career, placement and project guidance needs.
- **BO-02:** Provide centralized access to relevant resources and mentorship.
- **BO-03:** Enable seniors/alumni to identify students needing assistance.
- **BO-04:** Enable alumni to support multiple students efficiently.
- **BO-05:** Enable authorized organizers to publish relevant events and opportunities.

## 4. Scope
### In Scope
- Mentorship requests
- Connections between students, seniors and alumni
- Resource sharing
- Mentorship sessions
- Event/opportunity publishing

### Out of Scope
- Physical/in-person mentorship coordination
- General advertising
- Academic eligibility/access decisions

## 5. Key Stakeholders
Students, senior students, alumni, faculty/college mentors and authorized event organizers.

## 6. High-Level Solution
The platform provides structured requests, profile-based connections, resource sharing, mentorship sessions and an event board so users can find and exchange relevant information through a centralized system.
